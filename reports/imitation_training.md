## Design notes

Two earlier runs from this session failed in instructive ways. Both are kept here so the design is not undone by accident.

1. **First dataset: 900 games, every decision kept (161k samples).** The policy reached 52% holdout top-1, but the value head memorized the training games. Its train loss fell to 0.007 while dev loss rose to 6.6, far worse than uniform's 1.39. Every sample of a game shares one board and one winner, so the network learned to recognize the board. Dropout and a lower value weight only slowed this down.
2. **Second dataset: 7,400 games, 15% of decisions kept (this dataset), one shared trunk.** Value overfitting slowed but remained, and the shared trunk dragged the policy down with it: 52% dev top-1. Detaching the value head from the trunk lifted the policy to 63% within 8 epochs, but a value head reading policy features learned almost nothing (dev loss 1.35).
3. **This run: separate policy and outcome towers, each early-stopped on its own dev loss.** The policy trains to convergence (epoch 11) while the value tower is kept from epoch 2, before it begins to memorize.
